Bio

I am a systems administrator and my day-to-day work sits right next to the infrastructure: Windows Server, Active Directory, Linux, virtualisation, networking and backups. I like to understand how everything works before touching it, and document it afterwards.

I do not see security as an add-on, but as part of a job well done. I apply hardening, review permissions, manage patches and read logs with the same attention I give to a backup. The eJPTv2 certification helps me understand how an attacker thinks and prioritise better.

Outside work I keep learning in labs, reading and testing things. I am interested in blue team, Active Directory security and automation. If something can be scripted so it is not forgotten, even better.
